# 0264 — A swaying crest wanders on its second harmonic

`sway`'s wave is the plain cosine read at a phase that runs fast and then slow, and the term that
bends that phase is the **second** harmonic and no other, at a share under `1 / (4π)`.

The harmonic is not a taste: a phase term that repeats twice a cycle is unchanged half a cycle
along, so the wave stays odd about its own middle and its mean is exactly a half by construction
rather than by tuning — the property every profile owes (0122's file). Any odd harmonic in the phase
moves the mean and would have to be corrected for.

The bound is what keeps it one crest. The phase advances at `1 - 4π·SWAY_WANDER·sin`, so a wander
past `1 / (4π)` turns the phase back on itself and the wave grows a second crest, which is a second
family rather than a deeper cut of this one.

And it is not `split` at a depth ratio, which is the pair worth checking because a tape's wow is
this same idea at a size a hand does not hear: `split` is a fundamental against a second harmonic,
and this wave carries no second harmonic at all and every odd one instead.
