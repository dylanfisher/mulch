# 0357 — A bench is deleted when its arguments land

- **Date:** 2026-09-11
- **Status:** accepted, closing
  [0347](0347-the-marks-bench-is-its-own-route.md) and returning the fifth screen it added to
  [0247](0247-a-sketch-is-drawn-in-the-real-tokens-and-thrown-away.md)

All eight of the marks bench's entries have landed in the painter — the ground's push (0348), the
delay's echoes and the reverb's bloom (0349), the sound's rows (0350), the rack's second lattice
(0351), the scatter of big marks (0352), a landing's push down its loop (0355) and the part's own
alphabet (0356) — so the bench goes whole and not empty: `src/ui/sketch/marks/`,
`src/ui/sketch/MarksPage.tsx` and its test, `MARKS_ROUTE`, the `"marks"` member of `Route`, the
`App` branch and the View menu's item. `routeOf` selects four screens again.

**The drift bench's tenth and eleventh go with it**, for the same reason and not as a tidy-up: the
film's share landed as one dial a hand moves (0339) and the marks' ink as another (0345), and an
entry whose argument has landed is arguing with the thing it argued for. The two fields behind them
go from `src/ui/sketch/sketchDrift.ts`, which is the six it says it is again.

**A bench's graveyard is one list, on the page that still stands.** `SketchPage.test.tsx` names
every id the bench has been cleared of so a re-mount has to say so, and the ten new names join it
there rather than in a file of their own. The one name it cannot hold is `bloom`: the drift bench
mounts a `bloom` of its own — the shipped scene at entry 07 — so the marks bench's `bloom` is held
down by the absence of its files and of its route instead, which is what the case beside the list
checks.
