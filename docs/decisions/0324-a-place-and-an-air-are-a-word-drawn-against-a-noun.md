# 0324 — A place and an air are a word drawn against a noun

- **Date:** 2026-09-08
- **Status:** accepted, narrowing
  [0317](0317-a-yard-is-named-for-a-small-scene.md) — which held that a preposition is not a
  half of a name — and resting on
  [0149](0149-a-pool-is-sized-by-when-a-repeat-is-expected.md) and
  [0059](0059-every-label-is-titlecase.md).

**A yard's place and its air are each two banks, not one phrase.** `YARD_PLACES` becomes
`YARD_PLACE_WORDS` × `YARD_PLACE_NOUNS` and `YARD_AIRS` becomes `YARD_AIR_WORDS` ×
`YARD_AIR_NOUNS`, joined by `joinedName` at the one mint site — over `twoPartName`, which is where
two halves of any name are put together, so 0317's "the yard's join is its own" holds for which
banks are drawn and not for how two halves are spelled. 0317 was right that "beneath" is not
a half of a name on its own and wrong that it therefore cannot multiply: 24 places become 144 and
16 airs become 32, and a rack now repeats a corner of the garden without repeating a phrase — "past
the Stairs", "beyond the Stairs" — which is the thing a hand reads a rack for.

**A joining word is in the bank only if it reads against every noun of its family.** Per-noun word
lists are structure the name does not need, so the bank is pruned instead: the words are chosen by
reading the whole product aloud. Of the twelve prepositions a place could
open with, six survive — by, beside, near, past, behind, beyond — and the six that go are the ones
that ask something of the noun: "over the Stairs" is not where a yard is, "beneath" and "under" want
an overhang the Fence has not got, "along" wants a line, "at" wants a locus, "within" an inside. A
place noun is likewise something solid enough to stand on one side of, so no path is in the bank —
"behind the Stone Path" reads as nothing.

**An air is a medium, so it keeps two words and its nouns are made to suit them.** The air bank
holds a light and a weather at once, and no preposition of time reads with a light: "at Dusk" and
"at Moonlight" are one good pair and one bad one, which is the same half-a-bank failure as "over".
What crosses the whole bank is being inside it and moving through it — in, through — so the air
multiplies by two. Two of its nouns are spelled for that: "Dusk" is "Falling Dusk", because a dusk
is a moment and a falling dusk is a thing to walk in, and "Low Sun" is "Low Sunlight" for the same
reason. A scene keying on those entries (bench-06) reads the same light it was going to.

**The noun is what a later reading keys on.** A scene read off a name (bench-06) keys on
`YARD_AIR_NOUNS` and never on a joined phrase, so the join stays a matter of how the name reads and
the nouns stay the entries the instrument means. The word opens lowercase and the noun Titlecase
after any article, as the phrases did (0059), and the bound on durable text is unchanged: the banks
that always speak fit inside it over the longest word plus the longest noun, and the air and the
detail still give way to it.
