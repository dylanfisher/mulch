# 0197 — The card has a front, and the dials have a rank

- **Date:** 2026-08-28
- **Status:** accepted, extending
  [0195](0195-every-amount-stands-beside-the-dial-it-shapes.md) rather than reversing it, and
  keeping what [0124](0124-a-drawn-number-carries-the-amounts-that-shape-its-draw.md) said about
  _which_ amounts belong to which dial. Amended in three places by
  [0198](0198-the-front-is-what-the-card-opens-on.md): the fine tune is a fold, the run's bracket is
  an outline, and the picture says it is a control. Its "the picture is a control" clause is
  reversed for the walk by
  [0232](0232-a-picture-is-a-control-only-where-the-pointer-is-the-value.md), which keeps it for the
  ground.

**Nothing on the mulcher card is behind a press, and nothing on it is at the same weight.** 0195
put every number the module declares on the card at once and was right to. What it did not do was
give any of them a rank: forty dials of one size, in one ink, tiled with the box's own gap cutting
through the tint that was supposed to bracket them — so a hand met one flat field and the ownership
0124 declared was on screen at a weight nobody could see. The answer to forty controls is weight,
not doors.

**Three things carry the rank.** A run's dial is drawn a size up from the amounts that shape it.
The bracket is one node, so it runs unbroken from the dial to its last amount and the gap either
side of it is the gap between runs — drawn as the muted token's own tint here, and as an outline
since 0198, because that tint was the very token a knob's unturned track is drawn in. And a dial says whether a
hand has been to it: its caption stands at the page's own ink once its value leaves the switch's,
and stays muted until then. Most of the forty stand where the switch left them, so that mark is
what the card is skimmed by. All three are paint — every dial is turnable, focusable and reads out
the same number it always did.

**The card leads with the picture and the six names.** They were the two smallest things on it: the
walk drawn as a short strip, and the characters that fill every dial in one press behind an icon in
the header's corner. A press on a name is the shortest road from a loaded sample to a pattern worth
hearing, so it stands in the open under the picture that shows it working, with the amount beside it
and the reseed next to that — 0195's own argument for the arrangement's cast and the ground's clock,
said for the card's own door. The header is gone with them: a header kept for nothing is a shape
claiming room it does not use. What is left under the front is the fine tune, under an eyebrow that
says so — which 0198 made the fold it should have been, on this decision's own argument that the
answer to forty controls is weight.

**The picture is a control.** A drag across it writes how far a jump travels and how many bursts a
landing is cut into — across for the distance, up for the count, because that is how the sheet is
already read. It is the road the ground took: `player.bed` is turned by its dial and dragged on its
own picture, and both send the one field (0191). A hand asking "wander further" or "make it busier"
is asking about a shape, and the shape is on screen. **Reversed for the walk by 0232**: the ground's
rectangle is a place, where the pointer is _is_ the value, and neither of the walk's two numbers is
anywhere on its sheet. The walk's picture draws and nothing more; the ground's keeps its drag.

**The two casts are one vocabulary and two grammars.** The front's six names are presses that fill
the spec now; the arrangement's six are the set an auto-arrangement may draw parts from (0174). They
stay two controls, because they answer two questions — but the front is the one in the open, so the
press is what a hand finds first and the pool is where it is labelled.
