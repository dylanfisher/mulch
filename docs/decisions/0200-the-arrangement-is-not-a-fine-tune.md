# 0200 — The arrangement is not a fine tune, and it wears no box

- **Date:** 2026-08-29
- **Status:** accepted, and said again for the ground by
  [0217](0217-the-ground-is-not-a-fine-tune-either.md), which takes the second box out on this
  one's own argument. Amending [0198](0198-the-front-is-what-the-card-opens-on.md) by taking one
  box out of the fold it made, and keeping everything [0197](0197-the-card-has-a-front.md) and
  [0195](0195-every-amount-stands-beside-the-dial-it-shapes.md) decided: nothing is gated, and
  every number the module declares is still drawn.

**How a pattern is arranged is not one more question about the dials.** 0198 put every box the card
had under one word, and How It Is Arranged went with them — so the amounts that decide _which_
dials the walk is reading were filed under the fold for reading them more finely. A part hands the
walk a whole voice at its first jump (0176), and a drawn arrangement writes a run of them (0158):
that is the setting every other setting on the card sits inside, not a refinement of one. It stands
beside the fine tune now, on a fold of its own, immediately above the section it fills — so the
amounts that draw a run and the run they draw are one thing to read.

**And it wears no box.** A bordered box is what tells one question from the next inside a stack of
them, which is the whole of what 0173 built them for — so a lone box under its own word is a frame
around the only thing there, and a second frame inside the card for one run. The arrangement is
drawn the way the card's other folds are instead: the word inside the control and the caret beside
it, with the run under it and nothing round it (0106). `PLAYER_GROUP_LABELS` keeps the word — it is
still one of the questions the card is partitioned by, and one of them is simply not a box.

It opens shut, on the fine tune's own argument. What stands open on the card is the front, and how
a pattern is arranged is a question a hand asks once it has one worth arranging.
