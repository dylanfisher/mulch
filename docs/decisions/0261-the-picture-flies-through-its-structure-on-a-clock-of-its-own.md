# 0261 — The picture flies through its structure on a clock of its own

- **Date:** 2026-09-01
- **Status:** accepted, extending

  [0248](0248-the-structure-travels-and-its-identity-is-the-automators.md) and

The structure travels across its plane when the population turns over (0248) and opens into itself
over the band an age has earned (0251), and between two turnovers it still breathed the same breath
into the same part of the structure for ever. `fractalZoom` is the row's phase over the picture's
window: seconds, and the same journey every time it comes round.

**The flight is where that breath is taken from, and that is why it is not a wider band on it.**
`fractalFlight` is the deck's elapsed sounding over `FRACTAL_FLIGHT_SECS`, cut on the same ramp and
multiplied into the zoom beside the breath. `FRACTAL_OPENING` is untouched
and an age still says how much of it this performance has earned; what moves is the depth the
opening is taken from, so the same breath lands somewhere new at every pass. Raising
`FRACTAL_OPENING` instead buys none of that — it is one breath over a wider band, returning to the
same place every window, and a dozenfold in and out inside one window is a flicker rather than a
flight.

**A breath and not a dive, and that is measured rather than chosen.** An endless dive has to wrap,
and a wrap is invisible only where the structure repeats: a folded plane repeats exactly every
`ratio` of scale and an escape field repeats at no scale at all, so one of the two coordinates would
step from deep to wide inside a frame — the hard cut 0248 and 0249 spent themselves removing. Cut on
`fractalZoom`'s own ramp, the picture flies in and flies back out and its slope comes back where it
left.

**Seconds, and no count of the picture's own windows — which is the whole of why it takes no
period.** A window is recomputed from the longest row in the picture (`moireWindowSecs`), so a run
turnover that lays or retires a long row moves it; an unbounded sounding divided by a number that
moves is many whole turns of jump an hour into a performance, and the whole band inside one frame.
The one length this motion is a fraction of is the performance, and a performance is measured in
seconds, as `DRIFT_AGE_REACH_SECS` already is. Four minutes is a dozen of an automator's turnovers,
so the flight is the slow motion the travel is not, and several whole flights are over before the
picture is old. A flight counted in windows would also be exactly the breath's own subharmonic, and
a motion phase-locked to the one it is meant to carry is not a second motion at all.

**The picture's and not a row's**, for the reason its stops are (0248): the two fractal rows are one
structure, so one flight carries both, resolved once for a whole pass, and their beat stays the one
`FRACTAL_BEAT` gives them. `paintMoire` takes `sounding` beside the `age` resolved off it because
the age is the read's as well as the paint's and is resolved once beside the set (0251), while the
raw seconds are the paint's alone.

**On the sounding and not on wall time**, for `driftAge`'s reason: a paused instrument is not flying
anywhere, and one left open overnight has not been anywhere. A halt sends the sounding back to
nought (`DeckPeek.sounding`), so the flight lands back where a picture with nothing sounded stands.
Named here so it is not read as an oversight: that is the same fact as the age landing back on its
floor at the same halt, not a second one, and holding it would mean a clock the picture kept for
itself — which is the durable shape this deliberately has none of.

The shot that decided it: one yard, an automator laying a place every two seconds, shot at four
seconds and at fifty-five. Fresh, the picture is a straight weave; a minute in it is broad chevrons
standing over that weave, and the same minute with `FRACTAL_FLIGHT` at one is a tighter, flatter
lattice — swing 0.023 against 0.019 on the strip's own 1:1 crop, at the same mean. So the flight is
what opened it, and the structure still reads as a layer once it travels and is fed back, which is
the question the plan put on this step.

Cost: the fractal rows' tiles now step through `FRACTAL_ZOOM_STEPS` stops twice more per flight per
row — a few dozen extra picture-sized bakes across a whole flight, at one bake a painting (0144),
which is the budget the plan allowed the fly-through to spend. Nothing else moved.

Durable shape: none. `MoireRowSet.sounding` is the reading the age was already resolved from, kept
beside it because the paint spends the raw seconds too.
