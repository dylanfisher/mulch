# 0295 — The structure bench is its own route

- **Date:** 2026-09-02
- **Status:** accepted, adding a fourth screen to

A review of the automator's mark on the drift found it not so much faint as shared out: two rows
among about fourteen at the depth the count solves for, a folded coordinate borrowing the escape
field's centre and flattening to stripes, a second copy that is the first tile drawn twice a fifth
of the time, and a tile that stands still between bakes. The fix is not one number, so it is a
bench first (0247): seven moves, each the real escape kernel over the drift bench's stand-in weave
under one dial, at `#/structure`.

**Its own route and not a third list on `#/sketch`.** Every picture here is a pixel loop over the
real kernel at a hundred-odd iterations a pixel, and the sketch bench already mounts twenty
pictures. A page that paints all of them to show these seven is a page nobody opens. The route
costs what 0247 said a screen costs: one const, one member, one branch and one menu item.

**It reuses the drift bench's stage, frame and weave and adds no primitive.** What is judged is the
move, not the box: a bench on a different canvas would be arguing about the canvas.

Deleted with `src/ui/sketch/structure/` the day one of its arguments wins, exactly as 0247 says.
