# 0370 — The ground moves the loop while nothing walks

- **Date:** 2026-09-11
- **Status:** accepted, extending
  [0191](0191-the-ground-is-a-picture-a-hand-moves.md) — the strip is the control, and this is what
  it controls when there is no walk — and resting on
  [0183](0183-a-bed-is-the-loop-moved-and-it-is-the-transports.md), whose "the loop is bed zero"
  it takes literally; it leaves the bypass P164 decided exactly as it is.

**With the Mulcher off, the Which Ground strip is not refused: its plain drag moves the loop.** A bed
is the loop moved, and the transport folds it when it arms a step (0183) — so with nothing walking
there is no window to carry off the loop, and the loop is the ground. The strip draws the block a
hand takes hold of on the loop itself, and a drag sends `deck.loop` to the bed the pointer reached,
through the same `bedGround` fold and the same command the plant press writes a stood ground back
with (0185). Unzoned, because the loop is (0318). The loop moved is bed zero again, so a drag that
stops moving stops sending.

**Everything else on the fold stays refused, because everything else is the walk's.** The bed dial,
the period and the three words a move is shaped by, the plant and the kept row all author the
spec a walk reads, and a spec nothing reads is a control that does nothing (0195's reason for
drawing them refused, unchanged). The two modified presses on the strip — a kept ground, a zone —
are the same and are refused with them. The one thing on the fold that is the deck's and not the
walk's is the loop, so it is the one thing that works.

**A bypassed card still draws character for character as a card with no pattern (P164).** The strip
reads the sounding spec, not the held one, so the switch off over a moved bed draws the loop and
nothing moved — which is what is playing.
