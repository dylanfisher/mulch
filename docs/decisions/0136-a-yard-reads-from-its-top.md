# 0136 — A yard reads from its top

- **Date:** 2026-08-22
- **Status:** accepted
- **Amends:** [0107](0107-a-module-is-a-card-and-a-fold-never-silences-it.md) — the heading leaves the card, the switch stays in it

What a yard is playing is said once, at the top of the yard, by the control that changes it. The
generator menu ([P70](../plan.md)) absorbed the file field beside it and moved into the deck
header: one trigger, named `Source` rather than `Generator`, wearing the generator's own kind or
the name of the file the bytes arrived as — read back out of the id they are stored under
([0127](0127-an-export-is-a-folder.md)), so this is a second reader of that name and not a second
fact. The field itself stays in the tree under the name every route already used, off the screen
rather than hidden, because a hidden field takes no focus and the keyboard and the harness both
reach for it there.

**A card's heading may leave the card; its switch may not.** 0107 put both in the card's header and
this moves only the heading out, beside the seed the pattern unfolds from, the way the rack's own
section heading stands over its cards ([0106](0106-a-fold-is-its-own-heading.md)). The switch stays
in the corner every other card's is in, and reseed stands immediately left of it: a person looking
for what silences a card, or for what redraws it, looks in one place on every card. The two rules
0107 actually holds are untouched — folding is never silencing, and the switch is never under the
fold.

**A section with nothing in it draws nothing.** An empty clip rack was a heading over a frame around
nothing; the first capture is what brings it onto the screen. Nothing becomes unreachable by it: the
gesture that makes a clip is the yard's own ([0078](0078-a-yard-is-duplicated-by-one-command.md)),
and every other empty state on the screen already says something a reader can act on.

**Things at the top of a column start at the top of it.** A row that lays its items on the baseline
puts short controls in the middle of a tall row, so the transport's buttons and the jumps card's
two variations align themselves to the top of the rows they share with captioned dials
([0093](0093-a-knob-caption-reserves-two-line-boxes.md)) rather than the rows changing what they do
to the dials.

Durable shape: none. Every one of these is view only — no command, nothing durable, no history
entry (plan §2).
