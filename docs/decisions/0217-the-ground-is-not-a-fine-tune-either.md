# 0217 — The ground is not a fine tune either, and it wears no box

- **Date:** 2026-08-30
- **Status:** accepted, amending [0198](0198-the-front-is-what-the-card-opens-on.md) by taking a
  second box out of the fold it made, on the argument
  [0200](0200-the-arrangement-is-not-a-fine-tune.md) made for the first. Everything
  [0197](0197-the-card-has-a-front.md) and
  [0195](0195-every-amount-stands-beside-the-dial-it-shapes.md) decided still holds: nothing is
  gated, and every number the module declares is still drawn.

**Which ground the loop is read on is not a refinement of the dials.** The argument was already
written on the card, as the reason the box sat at the end of the fine tune's stack rather than
among the three a part carries: it is the one box under there that moves the window rather than
moving inside it ([0183](0183-a-bed-is-the-loop-moved-and-it-is-the-transports.md)), and it is the
song's and not a part's ([0184](0184-the-ground-is-the-songs-and-a-part-plays-back-on-it.md)) — there is one loop and the whole
song is read on it. Both sentences are arguments for it not being under that fold at all. The fine
tune is where one of the dials a press on the front already moved is moved on its own (0197), and
the front moves no ground, so the ground was never one of them. It stands beside that fold now, on
a fold of its own, above the arrangement's.

**And it wears no box**, which is 0200's own sentence said again: a bordered box is what tells one
of four questions from the next inside a stack of them, and a lone box under its own eyebrow is a
frame around the only thing there. So `PlayerGroup` is dropped here, the eyebrow becomes the fold's
own toggle, and the sentence that box's eyebrow carried (`PLAYER_GROUND_TOOLTIP`) hangs off the
`Says` on that toggle — the picture under it is still a canvas no pointer can rest on and no
keyboard can reach (0080, 0191). `PLAYER_GROUP_LABELS.ground` keeps the word: it is still one of
the questions the card is partitioned by, and two of them are now simply not boxes.

It opens shut, on the fine tune's own argument. What stands open on the card is the front, and the
picture, the strip and the kept grounds are a question a hand asks once it has a loop worth moving.
