# 0186 — The picture is anchored where the yard is reading

- **Status:** accepted, extending

**Where in the source the yard is reading takes the module row's `centre`.** 0142 put the anchor on
`delay.time` because an echo arrives from somewhere; where a yard reads from is the same kind of
fact about the whole picture rather than about one plugin in it, and it is the strongest claim there
is on that dimension. The declaration is `playerRowCentre` in `src/lib/playerDrift.ts`, beside the
module's other four, for 0159's reason: the player has no registry entry, so its reach is written
where the module is. Nothing joins `PlayerSpec` and nothing joins the session.

**What it buys is 0142's slide, not 0142's picture.** The module's row is linear and unswept, so its
anchor reaches `aim` folded into the one scalar its phase already moves along — it is a swept or
curved row whose anchor _is_ the picture. The step's own words were "the picture moves house", and
that is the thing to be exact about rather than to record: a straight row's anchor is a slide, and
what a slide buys is that this row's crests stand somewhere new against every other row, so the
product is a field neither of them held. Worth taking for that, and not for a composition
translating across the frame, which is not what a linear geometry can do.

**The crawl is `bedGround`'s and no picture may spell it.** The raw offset in sixteenths the
standing step carries is resolved by the one function the peaks and the plant already share
(principle 1, 0185). This is also why the per-frame read allocates: `bedGround` answers a pair and a
bound, so a standing pattern costs two object literals per painting. 0070 is paid everywhere it is
free — the rows, the reads and the maps are all written in place — and the alternative here is a
second author of the crawl, which is worse than the garbage. It is a painting's cost at the drift's
own cadence (0144), not a frame's, and the peaks already pay it.

**It is read off the step and never off the part.** The three fields beside it rest when the cursor's
badge names a part the arrangement does not hold, because a row cut by a part nobody stands in
disagrees with the song. A ground is not the song's: a step exists, so the yard is reading somewhere.
This is the one state in which the row's four fields deliberately disagree, and it is pinned by a
test.
